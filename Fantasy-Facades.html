Fantasy Facades

An endeavor of creative expression and a foray into 3D environment art

  1. Home
  2. Fantasy Facades

The fantasy facades project featuring an Elven-Style fortress and mythological elements came to be as a passion project combining a love of fantastical stories with modeling and design. Most architecture focuses on the production of pragmatic spaces; however, a designer needs to embark on creative endeavors and push the limits of creativity in design to expand their own skills and embrace their imagination.

This project served as an opportunity to experiment with form and style beyond general architectural practice. It is an exploration of design ability and self-motivation in conceptualization, developing, and executing a project that extends outside the constraints of traditional architecture. Additionally, the project functioned as a foray into 3D environment design used in media and entertainment industries.
